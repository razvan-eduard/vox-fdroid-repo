- Capitalisation is derived from what the field asked for and what is already written, so it comes back after you delete to the start of a line or move the cursor there, and it honours fields that want every word or every character capitalised
- The keys show whether shift is on, which they never did
- Two spaces make a full stop, undone by the backspace after it
- Sliding along the space bar moves the cursor
- A keypress can make a sound, off by default
- An emoji key beside the space bar opens a grid of 1,886 emoji in Unicode's own groups, with the ones you use kept at the front
