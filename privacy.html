Privacy Policy for VoxApps Suite

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the VoxApps ecosystem, including Vox Commander, Vox Notes, Vox Expenses, Vox Calendar, Vox Vision, and Vox Hub.

🛡️ Core Privacy Principle: Local-First / Private by Default
The fundamental principle of VoxApps is that your personal data belongs to you. VoxApps uses a **Local-First architecture**, where core processing and data storage stay on your device. Optional cloud integrations can be enabled for enhanced intelligence.

1. Information Collection and Use

VoxApps does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on remote servers. All core processing is done locally on your hardware.

  • Voice Data: Audio captured for wake-word detection and speech-to-text (STT) is processed in real-time on your device. By default, no audio or transcriptions leave the device.
  • Personal Records: Your notes, expenses, calendar events, and scanned documents are stored in an encrypted database (SQLCipher) strictly on your local device storage.
  • Biometric Data: We use standard Android biometric prompts. We do not have access to your actual biometric data.

2. Optional Third-Party Integrations (Cloud Features)

Users may optionally choose to enable cloud-based features to augment the local experience. These are Off by Default and require explicit configuration:

  • Cloud NLU (OpenAI/Gemini): If enabled, transcribed text of your commands (and optionally images for OCR) will be sent to OpenAI or Google for intent parsing.
  • Media Control (Spotify): If connected, the app communicates with Spotify's API to manage your local playback and search their catalog.
  • Currency Exchange Rates: Vox Expenses may download current exchange rates from public financial APIs to provide accurate multi-currency reports.
  • Search Providers: Web search queries (Weather, News, Wikipedia) are sent to the respective third-party providers selected by the user.

3. Data Permissions

We request specific permissions only to enable core functionality:

  • Microphone: For voice commands (Local STT).
  • Camera: For document scanning (Local OCR).
  • Notifications: To capture transaction info (Local Processing) or show active service status.
  • Files/Media: To store encrypted backups and attachments locally.

4. Peer-to-Peer Sync (NFC/Bluetooth)

Data synchronization between your own devices is handled directly via NFC and Bluetooth. No cloud middleman or central server is used for this process.

5. Contact Us

Since this is an open-source project, any privacy concerns can be raised as an issue on our official repository: https://github.com/razvan-eduard/VoxApps
